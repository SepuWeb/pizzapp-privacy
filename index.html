Privacy Policy – PizzApp

Last updated: 2026-04-11

PizzApp is designed to work primarily on your device.

What PizzApp stores

PizzApp stores the following information locally on your device:

  • pizza recipes and calculation parameters
  • recipe timestamps and active timer state
  • photos you choose to attach to a recipe
  • app preferences such as the last opened tab and review prompt counters

PizzApp does not require account creation.

Permissions

PizzApp may ask for access to:

  • camera, to let you take a photo and attach it to a recipe
  • photo library, to let you import recipe photos
  • notifications, to deliver local reminders for recipe timers

These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding app features.

Data sharing

PizzApp does not sell your personal data.
PizzApp does not use advertising SDKs.
PizzApp does not send recipe data or photos to a PizzApp-operated backend.

If you choose to use the share action inside the app, PizzApp can pass the selected recipe text and attachments to another app on your device, but only as a direct user-initiated action.

Data retention and deletion

Recipes remain on your device until you delete them.
Attached photos remain in the app's local storage until you remove them.
When you delete a photo from PizzApp, the associated local file is also deleted from the app storage area.

Security

PizzApp stores recipe data locally using platform storage available to the app. No method can guarantee absolute security, but PizzApp is designed to minimize data movement by keeping recipe content on-device whenever possible.

Contact

For privacy questions related to PizzApp, write to info@coleottero.it.
